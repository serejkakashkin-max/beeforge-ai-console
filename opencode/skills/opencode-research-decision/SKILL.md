# Research and decisions

State the decision, constraints, and success criteria first. Separate verified evidence from assumptions. Compare only viable options against the requested trade-offs, including operational cost and risk where relevant.

Use Sequential Thinking for structure, but keep the reasoning bounded and finish with a recommendation, confidence level, risks, and validation plan. Do not edit files, invoke external mutations, or turn a recommendation into implementation without a new request.
